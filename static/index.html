ACADEMIC & RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

AI Models, Datasets & Training Hub

Implementation of enhanced YOLOv8 with Deformable Attention Transformer, GSConv slim-neck, and MPDIoU loss (Yiwen Jiang 2024, Discover Applied Sciences).

ENHANCED YOLOV8 ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATIONS

Architectural Innovations & Benchmark

1. C2f_DAttn Module: Integrates Deformable Attention Transformer (DAT) into YOLOv8's C2f block to dynamically focus on irregular crack geometries.
2. VoVGSCSP Slim-Neck: Integrates GSConv grouped separable convolutions to reduce model parameters to 11.8 MB at 369 FPS.
3. MPDIoU Loss: Minimizes bounding box corner coordinate distances for faster regression convergence.

DATASET REPOSITORY

• 47,420 road images from India, Japan, Czech Republic, US, Norway, China.
• 55,000+ annotations across 4 damage types (D00, D10, D20, D40).
• Extended multi-defect dataset pipeline for streetlights, garbage & drainage.
